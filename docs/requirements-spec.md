## 1. Objective

The objective of this Proof of Concept (POC) is to build an AI-powered FAQ chatbot that answers Level 3 cohort-content questions for Gen Academy students using a Large Language Model (LLM) and an approved Gen Academy knowledge base.

The chatbot should function as a virtual teaching assistant capable of understanding natural language questions and providing accurate, source-backed responses. The system must answer only from the approved knowledge base and must never generate information using external knowledge or assumptions.

The POC aims to validate that students can retrieve course information quickly without manually searching through Discord, course notes, recordings, or documentation.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Students frequently ask recurring questions during the cohort, such as:

- Where is the Week 2 recording?
- What was covered in the RAG session?
- Which session discussed chunking strategies?
- Who was the guest speaker this week?
- Where can I find the session slides?

Although the answers already exist within Gen Academy resources, they are scattered across multiple platforms, including session recordings, notes, Discord channels, and course documentation. This creates unnecessary friction for students and increases repetitive support requests for instructors and teaching assistants.

The purpose of this POC is to demonstrate that students can ask these questions in natural language and receive immediate, accurate, and source-backed responses.

---

## 3. Scope

### 3.1 In Scope

The following functionality is included within the POC:

- Support for Level 3 cohort-content questions only.
- Natural language conversations with students.
- LLM-generated responses grounded exclusively in approved Gen Academy course material.
- Retrieval of answers from the approved knowledge base.
- Source citation for every factual answer.
- Short conversational follow-up questions within the same session.
- Discord webhook notifications for unanswered questions.

### 3.2 Out of Scope

The following features are intentionally excluded from the POC:

- Student authentication or verification.
- Personal student information.
- Assignment submission status.
- Grades and academic records.
- Payments or billing information.
- Refund requests.
- Human ticket-routing systems.
- Cross-platform conversation memory.
- Administrative dashboards.
- General internet knowledge or web search.
- No document chunking.
- No embedding generation.
- No vector database (e.g., Pinecone, FAISS, ChromaDB).
- No semantic retrieval or similarity search.
- A single approved knowledge base document is passed directly to the LLM for every query, rather than retrieved in pieces.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Primary Users

The primary users of the system are:

- Students enrolled in an active Gen Academy cohort.

The chatbot is designed to support students during their learning journey by helping them quickly locate course-related information.

## 5. Functional Requirements

### FR-1: Natural Language Questions

The chatbot shall allow students to ask questions in plain English without requiring predefined commands or keywords.

**Example Questions**

- Where was chunking discussed?
- What did we learn in Week 2?
- Where can I find the recording?
- Which lecture covered embeddings?

### FR-2: Knowledge-Based Responses

The chatbot shall generate responses using only the approved Gen Academy knowledge base.

The chatbot must not answer questions using external knowledge or prior model knowledge.

If the requested information cannot be found, the chatbot shall respond:

> "I couldn't find this information in the current course material."

### FR-3: Source Citation

Every factual response generated by the chatbot shall include at least one reference to the original course material.

**Example**

Source

- Week 2 Notes
- Session Recording (24:18)

The source should enable the student to locate the original material.

### FR-4: Conversation Context

The chatbot shall maintain conversational context during the active session.

Students should be able to ask follow-up questions without repeating previous context.

**Example**

Student: "Where is the Week 2 recording?"

Bot: Provides the recording link.

Student: "What about Week 3?"

The chatbot should understand that the second question refers to the recording.

### FR-5: Discord Escalation

If the chatbot cannot confidently answer a question, it shall notify the Gen Academy support team through a Discord webhook.

The webhook payload shall include:

- Student question
- Timestamp
- Reason for escalation ("No matching knowledge found")

This ensures unanswered questions can later be reviewed and incorporated into the knowledge base.

---

## 6. Non-Functional Requirements

The chatbot shall satisfy the following quality requirements:

- Average response time should be less than 10 seconds.
- Responses should be concise and easy to understand.
- Hallucinated answers are not acceptable.
- Only approved course material may be used.
- Every factual answer should include a reliable source citation.
- The system should remain stable even when an answer cannot be found.

---

## 7. Knowledge Base

The initial knowledge base will consist of approved Gen Academy learning resources.

### Initial Sources

- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- Session Notes
- Recording Metadata
- Guest Speaker List
- Course Schedule

### Supported Formats

- JSON
- Markdown

The knowledge base will be manually maintained throughout the POC.

### 7.1 Knowledge Retrieval Approach

The chatbot uses the complete approved knowledge base as the context for the Large Language Model (LLM). Instead of document chunking, embeddings, or vector-based retrieval (RAG), the entire knowledge base is supplied to the LLM so it can answer user queries directly from the full content.

**Workflow**

1. The student asks a question.
2. The complete knowledge base is provided as context to the LLM.
3. The LLM analyzes the entire knowledge base and generates an answer based only on the information it contains.
4. If the answer is not present in the knowledge base, the chatbot responds that the information is unavailable rather than generating unsupported information.

This makes the architecture LLM + full-document context, rather than a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline. See Section 3.2 for the corresponding out-of-scope exclusions (no chunking, embeddings, or vector database).

---

## 8. Success Criteria

The Proof of Concept will be considered successful if all of the following conditions are met:

- Students receive accurate responses to Level 3 cohort-content questions.
- Every factual response contains a valid source citation.
- Questions outside the knowledge base receive an honest "I couldn't find that information" response.
- Every unanswered question is automatically escalated through the Discord webhook.
- No hallucinated or unsupported information is presented to users.
- Students can successfully ask follow-up questions within the same conversation.

---

## 9. Assumptions

The POC assumes the following:

- The knowledge base is manually curated and kept up to date.
- A Discord webhook endpoint is available for notifications.
- An LLM API is available and accessible.
- Students interact with the chatbot through a web-based chat interface.
- Course materials are available in supported formats.
